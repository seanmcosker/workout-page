How to export from Apple Health
1
Open the Health app on your iPhone → tap your profile photo (top right)
2
Scroll down → tap Export All Health Data → confirm → share/save the .zip to your Mac
3
Unzip the file — you'll find export.xml inside the apple_health_export folder
4
Select export.xml below — everything stays local, nothing is uploaded
📂
Drop export.xml here
or click to browse — file stays on your device
How to export from MacroFactor
1
Open MacroFactor → tap More (bottom right) → Export Your Data
2
Select Spreadsheet export — choose the date range you want
3
Save the .csv file to your Mac (it may come as a .xlsx — export as CSV from Numbers or Excel)
4
Drop it below — weight and nutrition data will sync to your Lifestyle tracker
📂
Drop MacroFactor CSV here
or click to browse — file stays on your device
